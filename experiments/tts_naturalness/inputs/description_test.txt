The forest was silent. Not the empty silence of a place where nothing lives, but the heavy, waiting silence of a place where everything is alive and watching. The trees stood close together, their branches interlocking overhead so that only thin slants of sunlight penetrated down to the mossy floor. Every step was silent. The air smelled of damp earth and pine resin and something else, something old and sharp and alive.

Far away, a bird called once, and then was silent. Somewhere in the undergrowth, something small moved quickly away. I stood still, and listened. There was no wind. No sound of insects. Just that endless, breathing silence. And then, very softly, so that at first I thought I had imagined it, I heard it. A footstep. Not on the path. To my left. In the trees. And then another. Coming closer. Very slowly. Very carefully. As if whoever it was knew exactly where I was, and was in no hurry at all.
